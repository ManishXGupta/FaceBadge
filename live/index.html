How this protects people
  • Only attendees who registered with consent can be recognized. Anyone else is never matched.
  • Photos never leave your phone. Only one 128-number template per photo is used, for the closest face only.
  • Every scan is tied to the scanner's signed-in account and shown to the person scanned.
  • You can block a scanner or withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawal deletes your template immediately.
  • The organizer deletes all face data when the event ends.
