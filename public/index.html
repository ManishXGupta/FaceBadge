How this protects people
  • Consent once, at registration. Only people who registered with consent can be found. Everyone else is never matched.
  • On-device first. Video frames stay on the phone. Only the closest face becomes a template; other faces are masked and dropped.
  • Verified scanners. Only registered attendees can scan, and every scan is shown to the person scanned.
  • Your choice of sharing. Everyone, an AI agent applying your rule, or name only.
  • Block and withdraw. Block any scanner, or withdraw to delete your template at once. All data is wiped when the event ends.
